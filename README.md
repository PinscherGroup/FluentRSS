# FluentRSS

> A modern WinUI 3 RSS reader for Windows, powered by the FreshRSS Google Reader–compatible API.

**Your feeds. Your focus.**

Bring the power of FreshRSS to your Windows desktop with a clean, fast, and effortless RSS reading experience.

Designed with WinUI 3, FluentRSS gives you a focused way to follow the websites and publications that matter most. Synchronise your subscriptions with FreshRSS, explore the latest stories, and manage your reading list in an elegant interface built for Windows.

## Everything You Need to Stay Informed

- Connect securely to your FreshRSS account
- Synchronise feeds, folders, articles, and reading status
- Browse your subscriptions in a clean, intuitive layout
- View unread and recently published articles at a glance
- Mark articles as read or unread with ease
- Enjoy a distraction-free reading experience
- Keep your reading in sync across supported FreshRSS clients

> **No algorithms. No noise.**

Just the content you choose, brought together in one refined desktop experience.

Take control of your reading with a fast, modern FreshRSS companion for Windows.

**Requirement:** Access to a FreshRSS server.
